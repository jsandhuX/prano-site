Barcode, label, plate or menu

A packet has a barcode. A restaurant has a menu. A home-cooked plate has neither. Prano reads all four — barcode lookup, OCR on the nutrition panel, and vision on the food itself.

The same jar of pasta sauce scores 78 for one person and 31 for another. Your goals, allergies and conditions are inputs to the calculation, not a disclaimer under it.

The arithmetic produces the number. The AI writes the explanation underneath and never moves the number. Ask it anything — it already knows your profile and your last scan.

A score with no next step is trivia. Prano suggests alternatives that score higher for you specifically, and tells you what actually changes if you switch.

The score

Arithmetic,
not a vibe.

Prano's score is computed the same way every time from what the label actually says. It is reproducible, and it can be explained line by line.

0out of 100
Missing data is missing, never zero. Where a value is not declared, Prano estimates it from the ingredient list, says so, and lowers its own confidence.

Seven nutrients are weighted, then penalties compound at 0.85× because bad news stacks faster than good news. Then five structural caps stop a weighted average from quietly hiding a decisive problem:

An allergen on your profileScore 0
Conflicts with your stated dietMax 20
Industrial trans fatMax 30
One serving ≥80% of a daily ceilingMax 39
Ultra-processed (NOVA 4)Max 69
70 – 100

Good for you — fits your profile today.

40 – 69

Caution — fine occasionally, with something to know.

Below 40

Avoid — a real conflict with your profile.

The arithmetic floors at 1, never 0 — so 0 always means "this contains your allergen" and never "this is very sugary". If the most important signal in the app were also the signal for a fizzy drink, people would learn to ignore it.

Features

The same product does not
score the same for everyone.

A wholegrain loaf is a good choice for most people and a hard no if you are coeliac. Most apps give you one number and leave you to work out whether it applies to you.

Your profile is in the maths

Goals, allergies, intolerances, conditions and dietary choices are inputs to the calculation. Change one and every score in your history recalculates.

Allergens are flagged first, always

Allergen detection is never behind the paywall and never buried below the fold. Safety is not a feature to sell.

Ask it anything

The assistant already knows your profile and your last scan. No re-explaining yourself.

Trends that mean something

Weekly averages, what moved them, and which repeat purchases are quietly dragging you down.

A better swap

Alternatives that score higher for you, with the actual delta — not a generic "try wholegrain".
