Support

Real replies from a small team. If something in Prano is wrong — a score that looks off, an ingredient explained badly, an allergen missed — we want to know.

Email us

support@pranoapp.com — general help, bugs, billing and feature requests, plus data access, correction and deletion requests and anything else covered by the Privacy Policy.

Reporting a wrong score or a bad explanation

Tell us the product name and barcode if you have it, and what you expected instead. Product data comes from public databases and manufacturers reformulate constantly, so corrections genuinely help — yours and everyone else's.

Subscriptions and refunds

Prano Pro is billed by Apple, not by us. Manage or cancel it in your device's subscription settings; refunds are requested through Apple. The Terms of Service set this out in full.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, under Profile. If you no longer have the app installed, use the account deletion page.

Allergies and medical questions

Prano is general wellness information, not medical advice, and it must never be relied on for allergen safety — always check the pack. For anything about your health, talk to a qualified professional.

A word about allergies. Prano flags allergens it can find on the label, and it is careful about it — but a label can be wrong and cross-contamination is not on the label at all. Always check the pack yourself. Prano is general wellness information. It is not medical advice and it is not a medical device.
